What’s free—and what needs a key?+

The app and room preview require no subscription. Generate questions with your Gemini key, or import questions you prepared elsewhere. Your AI provider may charge for generation or apply usage limits.

What happens to my key and voice?+

Your Gemini key is stored in this browser and sent to Google with your prompt during generation. Choose tab-session storage or use “Remove API key only” in the app without clearing history. Browser speech services may process audio online. The landing preview does not request a microphone or camera and does not record you.

Can I return to my last practice?+

Saved sessions are available in the same browser and device. Export results and download recordings separately to keep a backup. Clearing site data removes local practice history.

Can I practise my own topic?+

Yes. Generate or import questions for your role, subject, or experience level. Choose quizzes, audio, or video practice.
